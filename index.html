Build More Robots.

THESIS

BMR designs practical robot kits that are low cost and practical for students, hobbyists, and researchers who need quick algorithm → hardware → deployment cycles.

Most robotics platforms force a choice between a toy that can't run real code and a research rig that costs five figures and takes months to source. Neither lets you test an idea this week.

Hardware should be the solved part. Everything we build is open, cheap enough to break, and ready to run your algorithm on day one.
